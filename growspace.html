GROWSPACE

An initiative of iedc-cetly to provide a workspace for the students of the college

Growspace is a creative student incubator and dedicated collaborative workspace at College of Engineering Thalassery. Whether you're hacking on a software project, prototyping IoT hardware, preparing for hackathons, or building your own startup, Growspace provides the tools, power, internet, and community you need to thrive.

// WORKSPACE INFRASTRUCTURE

BUILT FOR INNOVATORS

Free access to workspace infrastructure for all CET Thalassery engineering students.

01 // WORKSTATIONS

DESK SPACE & POWER

Equipped with ergonomic seating, individual power sockets, high-speed Wi-Fi, and a distraction-free environment for extended coding and research sessions.

02 // HARDWARE LAB

PROTOTYPING & IOT

Access microcontroller boards, sensors, measurement tools, and hardware kits to develop embedded systems, robotics, and physical computing projects.

03 // COLLABORATION

TEAM BRAINSTORMING

Whiteboards, display units, and flexible group seating designed for team discussions, sprint planning, ideation meetings, and hackathon prep.

04 // INCUBATION

MENTORSHIP & GRANTS

Direct connectivity with IEDC faculty coordinators, senior mentors, KSUM seed funding avenues, and Technology Business Incubator (TBI) assistance.

// WORKSPACE PROTOCOL

  • Log your entry upon arriving at Growspace and specify your intended stay duration.
  • Keep your designated workspace tidy and dispose of any waste in bins.
  • Handle electronic equipment, test devices, and cables with caution.
  • Maintain a collaborative, respectful atmosphere for all students.
  • Power down monitors and unplug chargers when concluding your session.
