The Innovation and Entrepreneurship Development Centre (IEDC) at College of Engineering Thalassery is an initiative to promote innovation and entrepreneurship among students. We provide technical guidance, mentorship, workspace infrastructure, and seed support to turn student ideas into real-world ventures.
