The Innovation and Entrepreneurship Development Centre (IEDC) at College of Engineering Thalassery is an initiative to promote innovation and entrepreneurship among students. We provide technical guidance, mentorship, workspace infrastructure, and seed support to turn student ideas into real-world ventures.

// CORE PURPOSE & VISION

OUR MISSION

Empowering students to turn groundbreaking ideas into sustainable technological ventures.

01 // INNOVATION

Cultivate Creative Engineering

Provide technical guidance, hackathons, and Maker Lab infrastructure to foster hands-on problem solving and technological innovation on campus.

02 // ENTREPRENEURSHIP

Incubate Student Ventures

Bridge the gap between ideation and market-ready products by supporting student startups with seed support, business modeling, and patent assistance.

03 // MENTORSHIP

Ecosystem & Growth

Connect aspiring student founders with industry leaders, Kerala Startup Mission (KSUM) networks, and alumni mentors for startup acceleration.
